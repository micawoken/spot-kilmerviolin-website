## Security Policy

Last updated June 16, 2026

### Overview

This security policy governs your use of the "Diversifying the Violin Curriculum for Private Teaching" website administrative services, accessible at:
- TBDTBD.com/admin, and
- www.TBDTBD.com/admin.

This policy outlines what measures we take to protect your information, how to report security vulnerabilities, and how we will respond to such reports.

### Security Measures
We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information. These include, but are not limited to:
- Authentication using Cloudflare Access, which requires you to sign in with Google before accessing the administrative services;
- JWT re-validation on all admin page visits and API requests, proving again the identity of the user;
- Row-level security on contributor records, ensuring authorization data cannot be modified by unauthorized users; and
- Encryption of data at rest and in transit by our service providers.

### Searching For and Reporting Security Vulnerabilities
You are permitted to search for vulnerabilities in the administrative services, so long as you:
1. Do not modify or delete any data on the service;
2. Do not steal or exfiltrate any data from the service, including, but not limited to, any data you are not authorized to access; and
3. Do not attempt to deny the availability of the service for general users.

All security vulnerabilities in the administrative services should be reported to the system administrator at EMAIL. 

When you make a report, please provide a detailed description of the vulnerability, including the steps to reproduce it if possible. 

### How We Will Respond to Vulnerability Reports
We will acknowledge receipt of your report with 72 hours and begin our investigation. If we determine that the report is valid, we will work to resolve the vulnerability as quickly as possible. We may contact you for additional information during our investigation.

While we investigate, we ask that you do not publicly disclose the vulnerability to allow us to address it. Once we have resolved the vulnerability, we will inform you and may publicly disclose the vulnerability and our response to it.

So long as you comply with the above guidelines, we will not take any legal action against you for searching for or reporting security vulnerabilities in the administrative services.
