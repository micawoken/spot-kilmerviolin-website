## Privacy Policy

Last updated DATE DATE, DATE DATE DATE DATE

This privacy policy governs your use of the "Diversifying the Violin Curriculum for Private Teaching" website administrative services, accessible at:
 - TBDTBD.com/admin, and
 - www.TBDTBD.com/admin.

When you visit TBDTBD.com/admin, we collect data to:
1. Provide the service requested;
2. Improve the service's functionality; and
3. Ensure the service's security and availability.

When you visit TBDTBD.com/admin, we may collect:
1. Information you provide directly to us, such as (but not limited to):
   - Data you enter into the database;
   - Lookup and searches you request from the service;
   - Files you upload to the service;
   - Login and contact information, such as your email; and
   - Any other information you choose to provide through the service.
2. Information about your requesting browser and device, such as (but not limited to):
   - your IP address;
   - your approximated geolocation;
   - any browser cookies you choose to transmit;
   - your user agent;
3. Information about your interactions with the service, such as:
   - the date and time of your requests;
   - the specific service features you use; and
   - any errors you encounter.

Information you provide directly to us is maintained indefinitely until you or another user requests its deletion. Data you provide and save onto the database is accessible by any authenticated user and may be included on public-facing pages. After deleted, the data may still be recoverable through Cloudflare D1 Time Travel for seven days by an administrator.

Information about your requesting browser and device originates from Cloudflare Workers Observability and is retained for no more than seven days. Data from Cloudflare Workers Observability is used to improve the service and ensure its security and availability.

Information about your interactions with the service also originate from Cloudflare Workers Observability and is retained for no more than seven days. Data about your interactions with the service is used to improve the service and ensure its security and availability.

We do not sell or share your information with third parties except to provide this web service.

As an administrator, you will authenticate through Cloudflare Access and its Google OAuth integration. By logging onto the service, you agree to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy, accessible at https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/, and Google's Privacy Policy, accessible at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

When you visit TBDTBD.com/admin, Cloudflare may set cookies on your browser. These are set in accordance with Cloudflare's Privacy Policy and generally are set to provide authentication and authorization.

We use industry-standard security measures to protect the information we collect. This includes authentication using Cloudflare Access, encryption of data in transit and at rest, and multiple layers of security and redundancy to protect against data loss and unauthorized access. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of your information.

For all inquiries related to this privacy policy, contact the system administrator at EMAIL.
