## Overview

The Compositions section lets you maintain the catalogue of musical works in the database. The
following actions are available from the [Compositions home](/admin/works):

- **Add new composition** — create a new work record.
- **View composition info** — look up an existing work by its identifier.
- **Edit existing composition** — change the details of a work already in the database.
- **Delete composition** — permanently remove a work.
- **List compositions** — browse all works currently stored.

## What is a composition record?
A composition record is the database's information about a specific composition, which includes who the composer is, what type of work it is, characteristics about the work, commentary on the work, and who entered it in the database.

### Managing credit
By default, the system will track who creates a composition and who updates a composition for you. When you create a composition, the system will automatically credit you as the primary contributor. You can optionally add another primary contributor and any number of additional contributors.

Primary contributors enjoy a special permission: apart from Administrators and users with the reviewer role, they are the only individuals who can modify or delete that composition. The contribution edit lockout will prevent any individual who is not a primary contributor for a composition and who is not an Administrator or reviewer from editing your composition. This does not apply to additional contributors.

## How do I create a composition?
Use **Add new composition**!

## How do I edit a composition?
Use **Edit existing composition**! As noted earlier, you must be a primary contributor, an Administrator, or a reviewer to make an edit.

## How do I view a composition?
You have three options:
1. Use **List all compositions** to show all of them, then pick the one you are looking for;
2. Use **View composition info** to lookup the composition by ID; or
3. Use **View composition info** to search for the composition.

## How do I delete a composition?
Use **Delete composition**. As noted earlier, you must be a primary contributor, an Administrator, or a reviewer to delete a composition.
